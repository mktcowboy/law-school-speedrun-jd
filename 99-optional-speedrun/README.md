# The Twelve-Week Speedrun

A fixed, compressed pass through the core of the JD curriculum: twelve subjects in twelve weeks, grouped into three phases. It keeps the original timeline for anyone who wants a hard schedule, but it is a companion to the year-by-year curriculum, not a replacement for it.

> Finishing a week is not the same as mastering a subject. Treat each week as a structured first pass or a disciplined review, never as the last word.

## Before You Begin

The speedrun works best after, or alongside, the traditional sequence:

- [1L Foundation Year](../01-1l-foundation-year/README.md)
- [2L Doctrinal Depth and Codes](../02-2l-doctrinal-depth-and-codes/README.md)
- [3L Practice, Synthesis, and Launch](../03-3l-practice-synthesis-and-launch/README.md)

For materials, the 1L and 2L reading guides control. The speedrun sets sequence and pacing only; it never overrides the [source priority rule](../00-start-here/resources/source-priority-rule.md) or the [open resource library](../00-start-here/resources/open-resource-library.md).

## What Each Week Contains

The subject weeks share one structure, so the cadence stays predictable:

1. **Focus** — the question the week answers.
2. **Outcomes** — what you should be able to explain by the end.
3. **Primary reading** — the open casebooks, rules, and statutes to work from.
4. **What to build** — the outline, attack sheet, or synthesis to produce.
5. **Suggested daily split** — how to divide the reading and writing across the week.
6. **Writing assignment** — a short written analysis.
7. **One supplement** — the single paid book worth buying, if any.
8. **Exit standard** — the test for moving on.

Week 4 swaps the daily split for research drills. Week 12 is a capstone: synthesis tasks, official bar-bridge resources, and a final deliverable.

## When to Use It

**Use it for**

- a second pass after the year-by-year curriculum
- a time-boxed review boot camp before exams or the bar
- a crash-course map of the core subjects before deeper study
- a deliberate compression challenge

**Know its limits**

- it departs from the ordinary 1L → 2L → 3L sequence of law school
- used too early, it rewards compression before comprehension
- each subject gets one week; real depth lives in the year guides
